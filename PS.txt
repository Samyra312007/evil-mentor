Problem Statement 1: Secure by Default                                                                                                                
                                                                                                                                                        
  Build any tool, workflow, or product that helps developers, teams, or businesses catch and fix security issues before they ship — making security     
  invisible and automatic.                                                                                                                              
                  
  You must use:
  - ArmorClaw — our open-source security claw that you can integrate into your pipelines and workflows
  - ArmorIQ SDKs (TypeScript and/or Python) — to connect your solution to the ArmorIQ platform for policy enforcement, intent verification, and audit   
  logging
                                                                                                                                                        
  ---             
  Problem Statement 2: AI Agent for the Real World
                                                                                                                                                        
  Build an intelligent AI agent that solves a real-world problem — monitoring, compliance, incident response, onboarding, or anything else — and show
  how AI can make security accessible to non-experts.                                                                                                   
                  
  You must use:
  - ArmorIQ SDKs (TypeScript and/or Python) — your agent must integrate with ArmorIQ for policy-gated tool execution, delegation workflows, or audit
  logging                                                                                                                                               
  - ArmorClaw — to add security scanning or enforcement capabilities to your agent's toolset
                                                                                                                                                        
  ---             
  Problem Statement 3: Open Category — Reimagine Security
                                                                                                                                                        
  Use ArmorClaw and the ArmorIQ platform however you want — build something that makes the internet safer, smarter, or easier to trust. If it's creative
   and impactful, we want to see it.                                                                                                                    
                  
  You must use:
  - ArmorClaw and/or ArmorIQ SDKs (TypeScript / Python) — your project must integrate with at least one of these to qualify
  - The ArmorIQ platform — for policy management, enforcement, or any other capability it offers
